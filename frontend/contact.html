We always prepare a 'buffer' portion (usually 10% extra) for all our events. However, if you expect a significant increase, please let us know at least 48 hours in advance so we can adjust our supplies and staffing accordingly.

Most of our freshly prepared dishes can be safely stored in a refrigerator for 2-3 days. For soups and stews, we recommend freezing them if not consumed within 24 hours. They can last up to a month in the freezer. Always ensure food is reheated until piping hot before serving.
